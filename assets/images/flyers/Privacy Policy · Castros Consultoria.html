Privacy Policy

A plain explanation of what personal data this website collects, why, who it is shared with and how you can exercise your rights.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short

  • We only collect the data you send us through the contact form, and only to reply to you.
  • We don't use cookies, visitor statistics, advertising or tracking pixels.
  • We don't sell or rent out your data.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data collected on this website is the responsibility of Castros Consultoria, based in Maputo, Mozambique.

  • Address: Rua do Palmar, nº 35, Maputo
  • Email: castrosconsultoria@gmail.com
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +258 87 866 5180

Our General Management is responsible for these matters. For any request about your data, write to the email above with the subject “Privacy”.

2. What data we collect and why

When you contact us through the form

If you fill in the contact form (on the home page and the service pages), we collect:

  • your name and email (required);
  • your organisation and phone number (optional);
  • the service and area you are interested in (required);
  • your message (required).

We use this data only to reply to your request, prepare a proposal or handle your registration for an event you asked about.

The form also has an invisible field whose only job is to stop automated messages (spam): people neither see it nor fill it in.

Sending the form is your choice: we process this data because you ask us to contact you, and you can withdraw that request at any time (see section 8).

When you contact us in other ways

If you click the WhatsApp, email or phone buttons, or our social media links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn), you leave our website and start using those services, each under its own privacy rules. The website sends them none of your data until you click. We only receive what you send us there.

When you simply visit the website

As with any website, your browser automatically sends some technical data to the servers that deliver the pages and files: your IP address, browser and device type, the page requested, and the date and time. This data stays in the technical logs of the services listed in section 4. We have no access to those logs and do not use them to identify you.

We don't use visitor statistics tools (such as Google Analytics), advertising, tracking pixels or any other way of building profiles of our visitors.

3. Cookies and storage in your browser

This website does not use cookies. It only keeps two small pieces of information in your own browser, which are never sent to us:

  • cc-lang: remembers the language you chose (PT or EN). It stays until you clear your browser data.
  • cc-loader-seen and cc-loader-handoff: used for the opening animation between pages. They are deleted when you close the tab.

As there are no statistics or advertising cookies, there is nothing to accept or decline. You can delete this information at any time in your browser settings; the website keeps working and simply opens in Portuguese again.

4. Outside services the website uses

To work, the website relies on the services below. When the page loads each one's files, that service receives your IP address and technical browser data, and handles them under its own privacy policy.

GitHub Pages

GitHub, Inc. · United States

What it does: hosts the website.

What it receives: the IP address of every visit, which it keeps for security purposes.

Formspree

Formspree, Inc. · United States

What it does: delivers the contact form messages to our email.

What it receives: what you wrote in the form and technical data about the submission. It keeps a copy in our account for 30 days.

Gmail

Google LLC · United States

What it does: the mailbox where we receive messages.

What it receives: what you wrote to us through the form or by email.

Google Fonts

Google LLC · United States

What it does: the website's typefaces.

What it receives: your IP address and technical browser data.

jsDelivr and cdnjs

jsDelivr · Cloudflare, Inc.

What it does: the Bootstrap files (the page structure and the icons).

What it receives: your IP address and technical browser data.

unpkg

Public service for open-source files

What it does: the library behind the animations you see as you scroll.

What it receives: your IP address and technical browser data.

Unsplash

Unsplash Inc.

What it does: the photo in the contact section.

What it receives: your IP address and technical browser data.

5. Who we share your data with

Only with the services in section 4, as far as needed for the website to work and for us to receive your messages. We don't sell, rent out or hand over your data to third parties for marketing. We will only disclose it to an authority if the law requires it or a court orders it.

6. Data processed outside Mozambique

Several of these services belong to companies based in the United States and use servers in several countries. So the technical data of your visit and the form messages may be processed outside Mozambique, under those services' privacy policies.

7. How long we keep data

  • Form messages: Formspree keeps a copy for 30 days. In our email, we keep the message for as long as we need it to reply to you and follow up on your request, and for no more than 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires longer.
  • Chosen language: in your browser, until you delete it.
  • Technical logs: for as long as each outside service decides.

8. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • find out what data we hold about you and ask for a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to our using it, or withdraw the contact request you made.

To do so, write to castrosconsultoria@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days at most. If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can also complain to the competent authorities in Mozambique.

9. Security

The website is only served over a secure connection (HTTPS), which protects data between your browser and the servers. Messages we receive are only accessible to the Castros Consultoria team members who need them to reply to you. No system on the internet is completely secure, but we take every reasonable step within our reach to protect your data.

10. Children

This website is meant for businesses and adults. If you are under 18, please ask a responsible adult to contact us on your behalf.

11. Applicable law

We handle your data in line with applicable Mozambican law, in particular the Constitution of the Republic and Law no. 3/2017 of 9 January (the Electronic Transactions Law). We will update this policy when Mozambique's new personal data protection law comes into force.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change the way we handle your data (for example, if we start using a visitor statistics tool), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change.

Questions? Write to us at castrosconsultoria@gmail.com.
